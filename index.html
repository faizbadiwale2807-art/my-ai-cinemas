Plan a movie scene by scene. Save your project on this device, export your screenplay, and organize your video and audio assets.

Honest prototype: this free starter manages your project and drafts scene outlines. It does not yet generate real AI video or voices. We can add free generation services in a later step.

Movie details

Suggested workflow

Start with a short test scene. Refine the character and visual style before attempting a two-hour film. A two-hour movie can require hundreds of shots and many separate audio/video files.

Scene manager

Each scene stores its action, dialogue, narration, sound cues, and a visual prompt for use in an AI tool.

Audio & video assets

Attach the clips or audio files you generate with other tools. Files selected here are not uploaded to a server. They are only listed in this session; use your phone's file manager to keep the actual files organized.

Save and export

Browser storage is convenient but may be cleared. Export a backup regularly.
